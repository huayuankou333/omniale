# Physical commissioning interface

The commissioning laboratory is an isolated native MuJoCo process. Connect to the Unix-domain socket `/cell_io/cell.sock`. Send one JSON object followed by a newline and read one newline-terminated JSON response. Responses are `{"ok":true,"result":...}` or `{"ok":false,"error":"..."}`. No candidate code executes inside the laboratory.

Operations are `status`, `reset`, `observe`, `step`, `camera`, and `finish`. `reset` accepts an integer `seed` from 0 through 3. There are twelve resets and 7200 simulated seconds across development experiments. `finish` flushes evidence; it does not assert success. Harbor stops development after 7200 continuous wall seconds and 500 model decisions, including waits/retries. Observations are unavailable after the collection freeze.

`step` contains an `action` object. Allowed action fields are:

- `seconds`: duration from 0.001 to 0.25, default 0.05; rounded to the native 0.002-second timestep, with a minimum of one timestep.
- `left`, `right`: optional objects with `q_rad` (six finite joint setpoints) and/or `gripper` (numeric 0 to 255).
- `cover`: numeric 0 (open) to 1 (closed), requesting the counterbalanced physical cover actuator. Its stroke is 0.50 m and station setpoint slew is at most 0.06 m/s. Observe the encoder and wait for motion to finish.
- `routing_clamp`: object mapping `left_0` through `left_3`, `right_0` through `right_3`, `left_rework`, and `right_rework` to numeric 0 (open) to 1 (closed). Each roof has a counterbalanced vertical slide and a carriage that parks behind the work area (0.80 m, 0.10 m/s, 30 N). Vertical strokes are 0.475 m for roofs 0/1/3, 0.200 m for roof 2 and 0.380 m for the alternate roof. Vertical slew is 0.06 m/s. Roof 2 and the alternate roof include descending side guards, an assumed 0.15 kg moving mass and a 0.6 N vertical actuator limit; other roofs use 0.06 kg and 3 N. Closing traverses while raised, then lowers; opening raises, then parks. Actual slide encoders gate each motion. These tools move by contact and create no wire attachment. Retract robots before closing retainers; open the selected branch retainer for rework. Roof coordinates and dimensions are in the fixture drawing and work order.
- `release_plunger`: object mapping two-pin part IDs to numeric strokes 0 to 1. These drive six physical latch-depression plungers, each with 9 mm travel and a 6 N actuator limit. They do not change latch flags or connector poses.
- `checkpoint`: `released`, `retention`, `cover`, `rework_begin`, or `rework`.
- `camera`: a camera name; returns a camera acquisition instead of advancing time. Do not combine with other commands.
- `finish`: true to end the replay episode.

The arm order is shoulder pan, shoulder lift, elbow, wrist 1, wrist 2, wrist 3. Joint units are radians, position setpoint slew is at most 0.65 rad/s, and manufacturer nominal joint ranges apply. The gripper drive follows the supplied Robotiq simulation description. Position servos and gravity compensation are simulation assumptions; they are not measured UR firmware dynamics.

Normal observations contain `schema_version`, `time_s`, `phase`, `arms`, `io`, `work_order`, and `simulation_status`. Per-arm fields are `q_rad`, `qd_rad_s`, `wrist_force_N`, `wrist_torque_Nm`, and `gripper_driver_rad`. Force/torque are the native flange-site reaction wrench in that site's frame, including the attached tool's dynamic/gravity effects; no payload tare is applied. Independent Gaussian sensor standard deviations are 0.03 N and 0.001 Nm. Encoders are nominal simulated joint readings. `io.cover_encoder_m` is 0 at full open and -0.50 m at full close. The first observation supplies the work order. There is no object-pose, grasp, latch, route or acceptance-result channel.

`io.routing_clamp_encoder_m` maps each retainer ID to its measured vertical position: 0 raised and the negative of its individual travel at full closure. The different parking heights provide clearance for the descending guards. `io.routing_clamp_carriage_encoder_m` gives the horizontal position: 0 parked and -0.80 m at the work channel. Tool position alone never establishes correct cable routing.

Each arm's normal-2 and alternate capture roofs share a rounded bend former. `io.bend_former_encoder_m[arm]` reports its `advance` and `shift` slide positions in metres. The former parks at X = +/-0.055 m, Y = 0.240 m, Z = 0.738 m. Its diagonal slide moves 0.120 m outward and 0.082 m north, reaching X = +/-0.175 m and Y = 0.322 m; the slide stroke is approximately 0.145341 m. A second slide adds 0.036 m north for the alternate route. Each axis slews at 0.05 m/s with a nominal 3 N force limit. Normal-2 and alternate closing demands are mutually exclusive. The station advances on the common clearance path, then selects the route before capture-roof movement. Opening raises and parks the roofs, returns the route shift to zero, then withdraws the former. Opening both capture roofs returns the former to its parking position. Wait for actual encoders before robot access. Former/cable contacts count toward the same 2 N aggregate cover/retainer pinch limit. These are authored nominal tools without measured hardware performance.

A camera request is `{"op":"camera","name":"overview"}`. Available names are `overview`, `metrology`, and `stage_<id>`, `stage_back_<id>`, `stage_front_<id>`, `socket_<id>`, `hand_<id>`, `mate_<id>`, `rear_<id>`, `rear_back_<id>` for each part ID. The result includes `rgb_png_base64`, `depth_npz_base64`, `time_s`, and `calibration`. Decode the PNG and the compressed NumPy array `depth_m`; depth is in metres along the optical axis. Calibration gives image size, `fx`, `fy`, `cx`, `cy`, and world-from-camera rotation/translation. Camera coordinates use +X right, +Y up, -Z forward; pixels have +Y downward. Images are native renders. Depth has independent 10-micrometre Gaussian noise. There are 160 acquisitions per episode. Installed camera extrinsics are supplied calibration, not object measurements. Visible colored fiducials are engineered optical features whose local coordinates are in the work order.

Inspect in order: released, retention, cover, selective rework. Open both grippers and retract all release tools before an inspection. The station holds for five simulated seconds without allowing robot support. Retention adds a 0.5 N upward physical load to each connector for one second, then removes it for one second. Cover inspection requires the cover fully closed. Each checkpoint is available once and does not return its acceptance result to the controller.

After cover inspection, `io.rework_job` identifies a branch and its alternate route portal. Request `rework_begin`, open the cover, physically disconnect that branch by at least 25 mm for at least 0.2 s, reroute it through the alternate portal, and reseat it. Every other connector must remain within its guarded geometric limits throughout rework. Retract the release tools, release the part, close the cover and request `rework` before finishing. The station measures cable geometry, termination constraints and cover motion itself.

See `SUBMISSION.md` for the final stdin/stdout executable protocol. Raw manufacturer inputs and authored fixture drawings are supplied; build native models and planning/analysis tools using the installed scientific software. The laboratory accepts joint/gripper/device commands, never object poses, attachment constraints, collision changes, material overrides or success assertions.
