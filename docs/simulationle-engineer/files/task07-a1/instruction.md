# A1 v2: dense-bin oriented feeding with inspection and recovery

Develop a closed-loop Panda controller with a contact electromagnet. Empty the
dense mixed bin into the correct receiving stations as released, upright,
oriented single parts. Recover every initial rework item as well.

The bin contains **12–16 parts**, generated by the original Task07 mixed
jittered two-layer drop-and-settle process. Occlusion, touching, nesting,
multiple pickup and hard final parts remain possible. The episode lasts
**120 simulated seconds**. Some episodes additionally start with one part
on the green rework pad at an unacceptable yaw. This is an extra item;
no original bin part is removed or relocated to simplify the pile.

Three same-color U-bracket classes have lengths 50.4, 72 and 90 mm. The
cross-section is identical. Effective mass is independently sampled as
0.34 or 0.68 kg. A heavy single part and two light parts can have equal mass;
neither class nor single-part status can be inferred from mass alone.
Use the calibrated overhead, wrist and inspection RGB-D plus noisy wrist F/T.

The three 14x14 cm stations are centered at (0.25,0.38), (0.43,0.38),
and (0.61,0.38) m for classes 0, 1 and 2 respectively, on the belt at
z=0.12 m. Target long-axis yaw is respectively 0, 90 and 45 degrees,
modulo 180 degrees. The legal inspection/rework pad is centered at
(0.30,0.54) m. Full geometry and calibration are in cell_spec.

Every accepted delivery must continuously satisfy all conditions for 0.60 s:

- Correct class station; body origin within 30 mm of the station center.
- Long-axis yaw within 15 degrees; U opening up within 15 degrees.
- Entire collision geometry footprint inside the station.
- Exactly one live part overlaps the station, including held parts;
  no contact with another live part.
- Magnet released; measured belt contact normal force >0.05 N;
  linear speed <0.02 m/s and angular speed <0.20 rad/s.

Accepted parts are removed downstream. Wrong-class, wrong-yaw, held, hovering,
flying, stacked and multiple-part deliveries do not count. A supported released
part resting in a station for 0.60 s while failing these conditions records one
rejected attempt. Lifting/moving it permits a later attempt. The legal rework
pad records no rejected station attempt. Parts remain recoverable.

The eight hidden episodes are assessed in four requirement groups:

1. Complete production: **all initial parts must be accepted within 120 s**.
   This includes the original dense-bin tail and any additional rework item.
2. First-pass quality: accepted / (accepted + rejected attempts) >=0.80.
   Zero accepted and zero attempts yields zero quality.
3. Recovery: every initial rework item must be accepted.
4. Safety: no floor drops, sustained damage-force events, tool-bin strikes,
   policy faults or simulation aborts. Original force/contact limits apply.

FullPass requires load/interface/stability/determinism qualification and all
four groups. SpecScore is the percentage of passed groups; it measures
requirement coverage. A separate **TaskScore** uses the original continuous
Task07 clearing/throughput/perfect-clear utility, including its nonlinear
hard-tail reward and penalties, on the stricter accepted deliveries. Complete
production alone does not maximize TaskScore: speed still earns credit.
Native per-episode counts, makespan, throughput and safety are reported.

Use make_policy() returning reset(cell_spec, seed) and act(obs). The policy RNG
seed is independent of the hidden world seed. At 20 Hz return seven joint
position targets plus a magnet command in [0,255]. At >=128, physical magnet
face contact welds every touched part at its current relative pose; there is
no distant attraction. Turning the magnet off releases all held parts.

Observations every tick: qpos(7), qvel(7), tau(7), ft(6), mag_on and t.
At 10 Hz: overhead_rgb/depth, wrist_rgb/depth, inspection_rgb/depth,
and wrist_T_world_cam. Intrinsics and fixed extrinsics are in cell_spec.
The original lighting, shadows, depth noise/grazing dropout, F/T noise,
robot dynamics, contact friction and magnet rules are retained. No object
IDs, classes, real-time object poses, segmentation or hidden state is given.
model_file is a robot/static-cell-only model for kinematics and dynamics.

Develop once for at most **7200 wall-clock seconds**, with unlimited resets,
experiments and tool calls; early submission is allowed. Submit
/logs/artifacts/policy/policy.py and dependencies in that directory.
The development container and its processes are stopped on return/deadline;
only the frozen artifacts are independently evaluated. No later human repairs.
Feedback control and state estimation inside the submitted program are allowed.

Resources are 4 CPU and 6144 MB. Each episode allows 360 s cumulative policy
computation, excluding simulation/rendering, and a 30 s per-act hang cap.
Independent verification runs two 20 s determinism smokes and eight hidden
120 s scenes: six bin-only and two with an additional rework item. A 21600 s
outer verifier limit covers CPU rendering; it does not extend simulated time
or policy computation. This is same-family generalization with new seeds.

Public practice example:

Add --video /logs/artifacts/dev.mp4 to record. The public harness implements
the same sensor, action, acceptance and force rules as the isolated verifier.
