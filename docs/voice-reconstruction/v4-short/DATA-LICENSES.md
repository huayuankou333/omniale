# Research showcase sources and attribution

This page presents a voice-replacement research rendition. The person on screen is the source performer. The target singer did not perform this video. Source and target audio are distinct performances. The model submission is preserved as delivered.

Acappella: Juan F. Montesinos, Venkatesh S. Kadandale, Gloria Haro, A cappella: Audio-visual Singing Voice Separation, BMVC 2021. Official dataset and original performance links: https://ipcv.github.io/Acappella/dataset/ .
