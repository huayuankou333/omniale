# Sources and research scope

## Acappella: The Circle of Life montage

Official source: https://ipcv.github.io/Acappella/dataset/
Original six-part recordings: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1mYF3ARkSKhV5a09G5MsefD7foFNRqHlt
Acappella research project: Juan F. Montesinos, Venkatesh S. Kadandale, Gloria Haro, A cappella: Audio-visual Singing Voice Separation (BMVC 2021).
This source is six separate overdub performances by one amateur singer, not simultaneous multicamera footage. Song credits on the authors' page: The Circle of Life; composer Elton John; owner Walt Disney Studio. The authors' dataset license retains original owners' copyright. Do not infer unrestricted redistribution or voice likeness rights from the availability of research data. This task is an internal research pilot; source media are not republished by this export.

Cite Zhiyan Duan, Haotian Fang, Bo Li, Khe Chai Sim, Ye Wang, The NUS Sung and Spoken Lyrics Corpus: A Quantitative Comparison of Singing and Speech, APSIPA ASC 2013. Original dataset notice follows in NUS-DATA-LICENSE.

## Organizer processing (v4)

Three original overdubs were silently excerpted to 54 seconds, anonymized and normalized to 720x720 / 25 fps. The guide is one clean 30-second vocal excerpt on its original clock; no artificial pitch change or time warp is applied. Target references are three 24-second excerpts from different songs. Video/audio mapping and original crop locations are organizer-only. These are separate overdubs by the source performer, not simultaneous multiple cameras. The target singer did not perform the source video. Internal research rendition only; source availability is not unrestricted voice/likeness or redistribution permission.
