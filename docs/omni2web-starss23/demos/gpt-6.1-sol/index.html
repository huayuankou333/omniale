ABOUT THE RECORDING

Real room. Real overlap.

A synchronized 360° room recording from STARSS23, by SONY and Tampere University. The original four-channel audio is decoded live as you turn.

A spatial preview, not binaural rendering

This horizontal FOA-to-stereo decoder uses W, Y and X; Z is unused. It is not an HRTF simulation. Play starts audio only after your gesture. Drag the panorama or adjust yaw; scrub to explore, or mark a segment to repeat.

How to read these annotations

Speech is strongly supported by automatic transcription and classification. Door and water estimates combine acoustic evidence with the scene. Quiet footsteps and brief laughter are more tentative. Directions, boundaries and moving paths are estimates, not official labels. Visible activity alone does not establish a sound.
