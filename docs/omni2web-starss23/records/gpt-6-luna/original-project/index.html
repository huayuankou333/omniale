How to explore

Listen: Press play to begin. The view follows the recording clock. Scrub to a moment; pause before selecting a timeline event. Drag horizontally or move yaw to rotate. Positive yaw turns left.

Loop & level: Set the marked start/end and enable loop. Volume is applied after the spatial decoder. Stereo is a simple horizontal preview, not HRTF binaural audio.
