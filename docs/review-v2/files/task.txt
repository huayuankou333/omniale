# Review Desk — delivery conform and technical handoff

Apply the authorized final decisions from the review recording to the persisted ticket system.
This is a controlled synthetic professional workflow, not a real client meeting.
The app works; your deliverable is business state, not source repairs or edited footage.

## Production SOP

- NORA is the approving producer. JAY is an assistant: his proposals are not authorized unless
  NORA explicitly adopts them. The active speaker is identified visually in the recording.
- Both players can contain valid media. The yellow ON AIR border identifies what was just played.
  Blue keyboard/bin focus may move to the other player during the spoken comment.
  "This shot", "this export", and "the shot I just played" refer to ON AIR playback, not keyboard focus.
- Picture notes (subtitle/crop/endcard) that say delivery/current cut must be conformed to the
  catalog's `release` version by matching the pictured shot in that export.
  The shots were reordered and retimed: old source intervals are NOT transferable.
  Use the entire matching shot, excluding black/slate leader and tail, in source-relative seconds.
- Technical audio findings apply to the exact export auditioned, over its entire technical-slate shot;
  do not migrate them to the release version. A clean export requires no new ticket.
- Subtitles go to editorial; crop/framing goes to editorial; ready contact/endcard graphics go to design.
  Contact/endcard work awaiting legal/brand approval goes to production and is blocked.
  Picture tasks are p2/open unless the producer says otherwise; urgent release work is p1.
  A framing hold stays with editorial, blocked. Audio faults are audio/p1/open unless placed on hold.
- Technical slate convention: the visual L lamp then R lamp should coincide with a short tone in
  the matching ear. A delay exceeding 0.15s is `av_sync`; wrong ear is `channel_swap`.
  Interrupted speech while the music continues is `audio_gap`. Do not confuse music with dialogue,
  legitimate percussion with a fault, or a preliminary ticket diagnosis with verified evidence.
- Keep original ticket IDs when explicitly updating old records. A withdrawn diagnosis is dismissed,
  not deleted; a different actual diagnosis gets its own ticket. Preserve unmentioned history.
- Later authorized decisions supersede earlier ones only within their stated scope.

## Available tools

Read the catalog/state, inspect the review recording or source exports, measure their audio, and
apply atomic transactions. Continuous contact sheets and source inspection are available;
you do not have to rely on the initial sampled frames. Independent ASR may contain errors.
Available issue types include subtitle/crop/logo/endcard/title/color/click/audio_gap/av_sync/channel_swap.
Use public_check for schema integrity, then finish. A prose summary is not a completed handoff.
