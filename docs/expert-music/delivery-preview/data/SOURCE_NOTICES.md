# Source-specific attribution

MuSP-Bench: Milan Liessens Dujardin, Song-Ze Yu and Kevin Miao, 2026.
The five piano inputs in this adaptation originate from MuSP-Bench's fixed release. Piano scores originate from ASAP/(n)ASAP, with score cleaning based on PianoCoRe. Selected recordings originate from MAESTRO through ASAP. Credits: CPJKU/asap-dataset; SyMuPe/PianoCoRe; MAESTRO dataset (Curtis Hawthorne et al.). MuSP original annotations/adaptations are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, subject to source-specific rights; source recordings and scores are not newly authored by OmniALE.

This adaptation selects original questions and their original media, adds Chinese helper translations, anonymizes local item IDs, reorders multiple-choice options and requests an offline learning website. Original reference-answer examples, symbolic performance data and answer keys are excluded from tested inputs. Composer/work titles are retained as learning-context credits; this is a documented adaptation rather than an identical original-paper prompt.

HumMusQA: Weck et al., 2026, official dataset DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18462524, question annotations CC BY 4.0. Individual Jamendo tracks retain their different CC licenses and artist/title credits listed in SOURCES_AND_LICENSES.md. Audio excerpts are the original published MP3 excerpts, not generated music.
